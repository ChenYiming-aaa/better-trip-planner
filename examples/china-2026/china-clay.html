Before you go

Emergency

紧急电话: 报警 110 · 急救 120 · 火警 119 · 高速救援 12122 · 全国旅游服务热线 12301(投诉/咨询)。非营业时间身体不适:直奔三甲医院急诊, 北京协和/西安交大一附院均有 24 h 急诊。药店密集,大象/同仁堂等连锁多有夜间窗口。

Safety

安全提示: 治安良好,深夜中心城区人流密集仍是常态;旅游治安联防点密度高。 Beijing and Xi'an are both very safe for a solo traveller at night by any standard; violent crime against tourists is rare and both cities' central districts are lit and busy until late.

Health & protection

11 月华北早晚干冷(0-4 °C),注意保暖与润肤; 西安/北京市区无高原风险。 流感疫苗与常规自备药(感冒/肠胃/创可贴)即可,无需国际旅行接种。

Holidays & crowds

国务院办公厅 2026 年部分节假日安排:元旦 1/1 · 春节 2/17 前后 · 清明 · 五一 5/1 · 端午 · 中秋 · 国庆 10/1-7(与 timor.tech 节假日 API 核对, as of 2026-08)。 行程 11/11-18 不撞任何法定假日,避开调休补班日即可。

Weather

Same dates last year (Open-Meteo archive, 2025-11-11→18): Beijing 4–17 °C by day, −2 to +4 °C at night, zero precipitation all week; Xi'an 6–18 °C by day, −2 to +7 °C at night, 2 mm on the 16th–17th. Expect a cold snap around the 16th–17th (Beijing dropped to 3.7 °C max last year on that date). Daylight is short: Beijing sunrise ≈06:55, sunset ≈16:52. Dress in layers with a windproof shell — the Great Wall ridge and the Xi'an wall are both fully exposed. Beijing air quality can spike in November heating season; pack an N95 if you are sensitive.

Money & payments

微信支付/支付宝扫码全覆盖,街头煎饼摊也不例外; 备 ¥200 现金以备山区/庙会等极少数扫码不便处。 景区、地铁、打车均支持扫码; 自驾停车多为扫码缴费。

境内无需任何特殊网络配置:地图用高德/百度(本计划的深链即高德), 打车滴滴/高德打车,=train 12306 App。行前下载离线地图包,慕田峪等山区信号弱时可用。

Decisions & to verify

Key decisions
  1. Domestic direct flights (PVG↔PEK) over rail for the two long intercity hops; Beijing↔Xi'an stays on the G-train — faster city-centre to city-centre, no airport dither.
  2. Xi'an first, Beijing second. This puts the Great Wall on a Monday and the Forbidden City on a Tuesday: the Palace Museum is closed Mondays anyway, its 40,000/day tickets are far easier to catch on a weekday, and the trip ends in the city that has your international airport.
  3. High-speed rail both ways instead of flying out of Xi'an. 4 h 10–5 h city-centre to city-centre beats a separate-ticket XIY→PEK connection (which would need a 4 h self-transfer buffer) and keeps one round-trip international ticket.
  4. Only two hotels for six nights — no one-night stays, no repacking mid-trip.
  5. The Great Wall day deliberately carries ONE anchor instead of three. Mutianyu is a 2 h ride each way plus 3–4 h on the wall; anything else on that day is a lie.
  6. Food is written as areas (Muslim Quarter, Gui Jie, Qianmen), never as named restaurants — Chinese street-food stalls churn faster than any plan can track.
  7. self-checked: 11 issues found and fixed — Monday closure of the Palace Museum (route reversed so Beijing lands on Tue), the 15/16 Nov winter-hours switch (per-day hours re-checked on both sides), Terracotta last ticket vs arrival, Temple of Heaven 16:30 last admission vs a 15:35 arrival, Shaanxi History Museum Monday closure, day-7 walking cut from 9.2 to 7.6 km, 6 hop rows added so every stop-to-stop transition carries its own map link, two lodging stops renamed to clear a name-collision warning, sunset prose aligned to the sun tool, the return-day clock rewritten as a formula instead of a guessed departure time; the international legs of the pre-adaptation data were rewritten as domestic Shanghai↔Beijing legs.
